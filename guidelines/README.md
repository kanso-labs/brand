# Guidelines

How to use the Kanso Labs logo. The brand page,
[brand.kansolabs.org](https://brand.kansolabs.org/), shows every rule here with
the files beside it.

## The logo

A tea leaf drawn in one continuous line, and the name KANSO in spaced Roboto
Serif capitals, ink on paper. It comes in four forms, each in full colour (ink,
`#1a1a1a`), one colour (black, `#000000`) and reversed (white, `#ffffff`):

| Form              | File               | Use it                                                            |
| ----------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Horizontal lockup | `kanso-horizontal` | The default: headers, documents, slides, anything wide            |
| Stacked lockup    | `kanso-stacked`    | Square and tall spaces: the avatar, a title page, a sticker       |
| Wordmark          | `kanso-wordmark`   | Very wide, short spaces, or where the leaf is shown nearby        |
| Mark              | `kanso-mark`       | Where the name is already said: an icon, a footer beside the name |
| Mark, small sizes | `kanso-mark-small` | 48 px and under only: favicons, tab and list icons                |

The small mark is the leaf with its line made slightly heavier, so it stays dark
at 16 and 24 px without its inside closing up. Never use it larger than 48 px,
where it reads as heavy.

## Clear space

Leave at least **x** clear on every side of a lockup or the wordmark, where x is
the height of the K. Around the mark alone, leave half the leaf's height.
Nothing comes inside that space: no text, no edge of the page or screen, no
other mark.

## Minimum size

| Form              | On screen               | In print      |
| ----------------- | ----------------------- | ------------- |
| Horizontal lockup | 120 px wide             | 30 mm wide    |
| Stacked lockup    | 80 px wide              | 20 mm wide    |
| Wordmark          | 80 px wide              | 20 mm wide    |
| Mark              | 49 px wide and up       | 12 mm wide    |
| Mark, small sizes | 16 px wide, up to 48 px | Not for print |

## Do

- Use full colour on paper (`#fafafa`) or white.
- Use the reversed logo on night (`#121212`) or another dark, quiet ground it
  reaches at least 3:1 on.
- Use one colour where only black can be printed.
- Scale the files whole, from their corners.

## Don't

- Stretch, squash, skew or rotate it.
- Recolour it, a product's colour included. The logo has no hue.
- Add shadows, glows, outlines, gradients or textures.
- Place it on a busy photograph or pattern.
- Rearrange, resize or redraw its parts, or put the leaf after the name.
- Retype the wordmark, or set it in another weight, width or spacing. It is
  Roboto Serif 400, but its spacing is part of the logo: use the files.
- Use the small mark above 48 px, or the regular mark below 48 px.

## Products under the brand

Kanso Labs is the maker. A product keeps its own name, and its own mark and
colours if it has them; the Kanso Labs logo appears beside a product only to
sign it, never inside its mark.

**Daily** has an identity of its own: a solid d on an iris squircle, the iris
palette and its own app icon, set in Daily's design canvas and shipped from
[kanso-labs/daily](https://github.com/kanso-labs/daily). It never takes the
leaf, and the leaf never takes the iris. Kanso Labs signs it as its developer:
the Google Play and App Store developer name and icon, and "by Kanso Labs" in an
About screen or a site footer, in Roboto Flex, in ink or muted grey.

**Kanso UI**, react-native-kanso-ui and the organization's other libraries and
tools are part of Kanso Labs rather than products with marks of their own. They
are named in words, "Kanso UI", beside the Kanso Labs mark or logo on their
Storybooks, docs and package pages. Their default theme is kanso-ui's own
palette; a library's demo colours are not Kanso Labs colours.

A new product that needs a mark of its own gets one drawn for it, like Daily's,
not a variation of the leaf.
